# Worked examples

## Single assertion

`synthetic_single_assertion.json` is a fictional structural fixture. Every scientific identifier, source and claim under `example.org` is invented for demonstration. It asserts that entity A interacts with entity B in a stated experimental context. One publication locator identifies the originating claim. The source explicitly reports the context, while the construction activity records its conversion into a PAR.

The two graph nodes and one relationship belong to the claim. The declared connection point allows the record to join another graph. The source publication and extraction activity belong to provenance and are not extra scientific claim components.

## Redistribution

If two databases reproduce this same claim from the same identified source record, the PAR retains one claim identity and records both databases as distribution cross-references. That reconciliation requires a verified claim match. Merely citing the same publication is insufficient.

## Distinct assessments

If two expert groups assess the same gene–disease relationship using different criteria, their assessments are distinct PARs. Both may link to the same underlying experiment. An application may compare their conclusions without treating two assessments as two independent observations.

## Minimally inseparable claim

A source may assert a pathway in which A activates B and B modifies C, with the combined sequence necessary to state the claim. One PAR may contain both relationships, an overarching claim, an explicit boundary and the source-supported sequence. If each relation is independently asserted and interpretable, separate PARs preserve those identities and can be joined into a derived pathway view.

## DDKG source recovery

A source-attributed AFF4–CHOPS edge can enter a candidate inventory with its exact predicate, SAB and observed identifier mappings. Origin recovery supplies the source record and claim locator before a source-grounded PAR is released. The synthetic example does not supply or imply recovered publication evidence for AFF4–CHOPS.
